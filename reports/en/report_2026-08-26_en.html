Diplomacy & International Relations

US sanctions on Iran and China's pushback

The Daily Telegraph, Die Welt, Süddeutsche Zeitung and The Guardian all cover the fallout from Bessent's dramatic threat of an economic 'D-Day' against Iran, but frame it differently. Die Welt and Süddeutsche Zeitung emphasize that beneath the bluster Washington is quietly avoiding direct confrontation with China over Iranian oil purchases, suggesting American weakness and a double standard where Trump acts cautiously against powerful rivals like China but aggressively against weaker ones. The Daily Telegraph instead casts the whole campaign as delusional overreach destabilizing global energy, food and bond markets, while The Guardian focuses on China's angry, legalistic rejection of the sanctions threat and the risk of escalation ahead of a planned Trump-Xi summit.

Sources: The Daily Telegraph, Die Welt, The Guardian, Süddeutsche Zeitung

Security & Conflict

Iran: Strait of Hormuz and internal economic crisis

The Guardian and Süddeutsche Zeitung both look past the military rhetoric to Iran's underlying position. The Guardian argues Iran's leverage over the Strait of Hormuz is diminishing over time, pushing Tehran toward a negotiated deal rather than confrontation. Süddeutsche Zeitung instead probes the gap between Iranian leaders' public displays of calm defiance and real internal warnings of economic collapse, noting that hardliners in the Revolutionary Guard believe they can simply wait out Trump's presidency.

Sources: The Guardian, Süddeutsche Zeitung

Domestic Politics

AfD, far-right rise and Germany's political 'firewall' debate

Coverage of the AfD's rise spans multiple angles. The Daily Telegraph focuses on the party's threat to shut down a public broadcaster, framing it as a press-freedom issue. Die Welt runs two contrasting pieces: one criticizing the AfD's aggressive 'Vorfeld' activists, and another - from Rügen's district administrator - blaming mainstream parties, especially the SPD, for ignoring migration concerns and criticizing both the 'firewall' strategy and calls to ban the party. Süddeutsche Zeitung dedicates coverage to the upcoming Sachsen-Anhalt state election, portraying the CDU's defensive campaign against a formally extremist-classified AfD and quoting an economist warning of the economic damage a xenophobic AfD government could cause.

Sources: The Daily Telegraph, Die Welt, Süddeutsche Zeitung

Trade & Economics

Canada's tariff retaliation in the trade war with Trump

The Guardian and Die Welt both cover the escalating US-Canada trade war. The Guardian, merging two related pieces into a single narrative voice, frames Canada's retaliatory tariffs as a calculated, unified response asserting national sovereignty - Canadians remaining 'masters of their own home'. Die Welt instead emphasizes the aggressive American side, quoting a Canadian official accusing Trump of trying to turn Canada into a 'vassal state' amid demands for Canadian 'subordination'.

Sources: Die Welt, The Guardian

Domestic Politics

US Supreme Court and mail-in voting restrictions

Süddeutsche Zeitung covers this story from two angles that together form one editorial line: a factual report on the Supreme Court's provisional decision to let Trump's mail-in voting restrictions proceed, and a sharper opinion piece accusing the court of missing a chance to resist Trump's unfair intentions and of damaging democracy ahead of the midterms.

Sources: Süddeutsche Zeitung

Süddeutsche Zeitung focuses more on the political optics of Merz's toughened rhetoric against hybrid-attack perpetrators, noting he still avoids naming Russia directly, a contrast with previous public silence.

Russia-Ukraine war and international support
