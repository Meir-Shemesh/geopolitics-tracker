Third anniversary of October 7 - mourning and Israeli political accountability

⌄

Coverage of the third anniversary of October 7 splits along several distinct axes. The Daily Telegraph, in a piece by Henry Bodkin, centers on the families of female IDF spotters killed that day and squarely blames Netanyahu's government for ignoring warnings and blocking an independent inquiry - a direct accountability frame. Where the Telegraph and USA Today frame the anniversary as an immediate political reckoning with Netanyahu, Die Welt pulls back to a longer-term societal and demographic lens, and the Los Angeles Times and Guardian each add, respectively, a public-opinion dimension and an international diaspora angle.
