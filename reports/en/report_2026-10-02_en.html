Flydubai co-pilot hijacking/attack attempt on Tel Aviv flight

⌄

Most outlets report the same basic facts - an Omani flydubai co-pilot allegedly tried to crash a Dubai-to-Tel Aviv flight before being overpowered - but frame the episode quite differently. Finally, in a Guardian column, Gwyn Topham broadens the discussion into aviation policy more generally, arguing that post-9/11 cockpit lockdown rules have repeatedly let rogue pilots crash planes and have cost more lives than terrorism itself - a systemic critique not found in any of the other sources.
