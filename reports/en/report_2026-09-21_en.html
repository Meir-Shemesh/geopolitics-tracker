AI race, safety debate and financial risk

⌄

Coverage of this topic splits into several distinct strands. On the financial front, The Guardian and Die Welt converge from different angles: the former warns of opaque, debt-laden financial structures underpinning the AI boom, while the latter relays Ray Dalio's warning of a valuation bubble decoupled from reality and mounting US government debt risks. Finally, The Wall Street Journal contributes a wholly separate educational angle, calling for universities to teach students to think with, without, and about AI, sidestepping the geopolitical and financial debates altogether.

⌄

The Daily Telegraph frames the story through a geopolitical lens: its core reporting centers on Chancellor Merz being weakened by a "humiliating double defeat" to the AfD and Die Linke, repeatedly stressing that the AfD is a Kremlin-friendly party seeking an end to Russia sanctions and Ukraine arms deliveries, so its rise is portrayed as a source of broad European alarm. Süddeutsche Zeitung concentrates on the CDU's internal collapse, dubbing it the "problem child" that lost governing power in Saxony-Anhalt and Berlin within two weeks and was reduced to a fringe party in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern under Merz's weak leadership, while likewise citing Schwesig as proof that a strong personality can beat back the AfD. Overall, the Telegraph situates the story within a Russia-Ukraine geopolitical threat, Die Welt is split between blaming the radical left and offering a social-democratic remedy, and Süddeutsche Zeitung zeroes in on the CDU's internal collapse and its socioeconomic underpinnings.

German fuel price and energy policy

⌄

Coverage of German fuel and energy policy splits sharply between Die Welt's single ideological broadside and three distinct but complementary critiques from Süddeutsche Zeitung. Where Die Welt frames the issue as one of civic responsibility and economic morality, Süddeutsche Zeitung consistently locates the problem in the government's own policy failures - both toward consumers and toward the industrial infrastructure of the energy transition.
