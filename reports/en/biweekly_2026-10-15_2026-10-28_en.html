Biweekly Trends Report

October 15–28, 2026

The past two weeks show two overlapping threads rather than one single story: widespread domestic unrest and fiscal/political strain - from French student and Yellow Vest protests amid a debt crisis, to Serbia's anti-Vucic movement, Alberta's separatism debate and India's youth protests - alongside a cluster of security escalations tied to Russia and the Middle East, including Trump-Zelensky/Starlink tensions over Ukraine, Russian hybrid warfare against Europe, Houthi attacks near Saudi Arabia, and Iranian activity in the Strait of Hormuz. Israel featured prominently across multiple distinct angles (Netanyahu's domestic troubles, West Bank settler violence, and Gaza war conduct), reinforcing an overall picture of leaders under pressure at home even as regional conflicts keep intensifying abroad.
