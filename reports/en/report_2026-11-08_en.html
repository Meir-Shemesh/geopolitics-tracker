⌄

Coverage of Trump's midterm rout splits along distinct institutional and ideological lines. Russia-Ukraine war and US support

⌄

Coverage splits between on-the-ground reporting and analysis of the shifting politics behind U.S. support. Los Angeles Times, in a piece by Derek Gatopoulos, Dmytro Zhyhinas and Dan Bashakov, and The Observer both focus on concrete battlefield events: heavy Russian missile and drone strikes on Kyiv and other regions ahead of peace talks in Abu Dhabi, followed by a strike that killed at least fifty people at an aid distribution centre in Zaporizhzhia. UK slavery reparations debate

⌄

Both pieces on this topic appear in the Daily Telegraph and both reject direct financial reparations, but they reach that conclusion from different starting points. So while Farage denies any British responsibility at all, Tan acknowledges a moral debt but redirects it toward practical, forward-looking measures rather than reparations.
