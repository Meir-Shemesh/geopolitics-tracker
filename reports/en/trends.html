Coverage Change Map: What Moved

Beta

Color shows how far a country's share of the coverage differs from its usual level, not how big the country is. Brown means rising, blue means fading. Countries with little evidence, in the current week or in the comparison period, are striped and left uncolored, so a jump from one article to two never looks like a surge. A country is also striped and left uncolored when the two ways of calculating its share point in opposite directions, because then we cannot tell whether it is rising or fading.

Beta: read with care

  • The count includes only articles that explicitly mention the country, so the figures are a lower bound.
  • A country's share is the average of its share in each newspaper, so a paper that prints more does not weigh more.
  • Do not read a trend into a country from a small share or from a single week.
  • The figures describe the newspapers surveyed only, not coverage worldwide.
  • Days of catch-up publishing are not fully neutralized.
