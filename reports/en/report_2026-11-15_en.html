Ukraine peace negotiations and Russian demands

⌄

The five pieces together sketch different layers of the same unfolding diplomacy. The Daily Telegraph focuses on the widening scope of Russian demands: in one article Joe Barnes reports that Moscow wants Britain to end its nuclear protection of European allies as a condition for peace in Ukraine, while a companion piece, co-written with James Crisp, carries exiled Belarusian opposition leader Sviatlana Tsikhanouskaya's warning that Belarus must be included in any settlement, since Russia could otherwise use Belarusian territory to stage hybrid attacks on Ukraine and Europe - together extending the story beyond the battlefield into the broader question of European security architecture. The Los Angeles Times, in Hanna Arhirova's report, centers on Zelensky's suspicion that a US-floated European security framework could actually serve Putin by letting him redraw borders and weaken NATO, stressing his insistence that decisions about Europe's security belong to Europeans themselves once the war ends. Taken together, The Daily Telegraph highlights the most sweeping Russian demands toward NATO and Belarus, The Observer and USA Today converge on the specific Donbas-for-NATO trade from complementary angles, and the Los Angeles Times foregrounds Zelensky's wary pushback against the American framework itself.
