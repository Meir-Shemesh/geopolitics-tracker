Biweekly Trends Report

November 12–25, 2026

The past two weeks show a clear split between persistent security flashpoints and mounting political strain at home. Ukraine's peace negotiations, US-Iran tensions and Yemen's war kept the international security agenda busy, while in Washington a cluster of stories, the Nvidia espionage scandal, White House-Congress oversight battles, criticism of Trump's governing style after the midterm defeat, and the Republican split over tariffs and JD Vance's future, all point to a presidency under growing internal and institutional pressure. Alongside this, worries about economic footing resurfaced through the US AI equity bubble and Fed policy debate, as well as Germany's VW crisis and defense-spending gap, suggesting that economic fragility is becoming as prominent a theme as the geopolitical one.
