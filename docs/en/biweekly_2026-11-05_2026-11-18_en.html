Biweekly Trends Report

November 5–18, 2026

The past two weeks were dominated by three parallel pressure points: the United States in the aftermath of the midterm elections (with Stephen Miller's appointment and the Hegseth impeachment push), the Middle East (the US-Iran confrontation's fallout, Israel's coalition crisis around Eisenkot and Netanyahu, and Gaza's humanitarian emergency), and the Russia-Ukraine war, where strikes continued alongside a renewed diplomatic track. Beyond these, a broader pattern of domestic politics buckling under economic and social strain runs through France, Poland, South Africa and Paraguay alike, while AI bubble fears and hybrid cyberattacks on European infrastructure added a distinct technological-security thread to the period.
