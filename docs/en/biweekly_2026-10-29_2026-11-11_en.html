Biweekly Trends Report

October 29–11, 2026

Over the past two weeks, two regions dominated coverage: the Middle East, with Israel's deadlocked coalition talks and the pivotal Arab-Israeli kingmaker role alongside the Russia-Ukraine war and its peace-talk maneuvering, and Europe, gripped by mass unrest in France, a constitutional crisis in Poland, German military buildup against Russia, and Russia-linked cyberattacks on infrastructure - together painting a continent under simultaneous security and socio-political strain. In parallel, the US midterms and their ripple effects across the economy, the Pentagon, and the justice system kept American domestic politics in sharp focus, suggesting a broader pattern of democracies on both sides of the Atlantic straining under political and geopolitical pressure.
