US-Denmark-Greenland security deal

⌄

All four outlets converge on the substance of the deal - a compromise preserving Danish and Greenlandic sovereignty while expanding US military access - but diverge in tone and emphasis. The Daily Telegraph foregrounds Danish derision, portraying commentators and officials mocking Trump's claim of a "historic" agreement as little more than a rebranding of the pre-existing security status quo, with one critic calling it a "spectacular defeat" dressed up as a victory. Los Angeles Times and The Washington Post instead adopt an American strategic-analysis lens, framing the deal as a face-saving retreat that lets Trump claim political credit while conceding he "did not get his way" on his real ambition to acquire or control Greenland, and both raise doubts about whether the resulting strain on US-Denmark and NATO relations was worth it. USA Today brings a more local and skeptical angle, highlighting Greenlanders' distrust and warnings that the agreement likely marks not the end but merely a stage in Washington's longer-term interest in the territory and its resources.
